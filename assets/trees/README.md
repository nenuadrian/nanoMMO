# Trees

The trees the client plants on the island. They're in git, as their licence lets anyone share them,
as long as they credit whoever made them.

| File | What | From | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tree.glb` | Tree, by Poly by Google | [Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza/m/6pwiq7hSrHr), as glTF | [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |

The file is as it comes from Poly Pizza. The client reads it as triangles, and colours each face from
the model's texture, a palette of four colours, under the middle of the face. It scales the tree to
five metres, and each tree on the island to between four and seven.
